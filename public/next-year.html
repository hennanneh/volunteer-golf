Colonial Country Club · Fort Worth, Texas

Why volunteer as a marshal?

  • Sit courtside with the best view of every player
  • The chance to play the historic Colonial course yourself
  • A behind-the-scenes look at a PGA TOUR event
  • Friendships with volunteers who come back year after year
  • Free tickets for friends to come by and say hi
  • A free beer after your shift
Volunteer marshals work four half-day shifts during the four days of the tournament, plus one additional shift Monday through Wednesday during the Pro-Ams and practice day.
